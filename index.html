Photo Pairs

This browser cannot edit folders. Open Photo Pairs in Chrome, Edge, Brave or Opera on a computer. (Safari, Firefox and phones do not allow it.)

Open a folder of photos

Photo Pairs matches every JPEG with its RAW file. Review them together, mark the ones you don't want, and delete both files in one go.

Tap a place to cycle: empty → ✓ copied → ✓✓ checked (you opened it and it is fine) → – not needed. An album is safe () when it is in at least 2 places and at least one is not the computer or an SD card; otherwise it is at risk ().

Import rows

Copy rows from a spreadsheet (or a CSV) and paste them here. Use one line per album: name, year, then one value per column. A first line with column names is recognised. Y or X becomes ✓, a dash becomes –, other text is kept.

Tap a cell to cycle: empty → ✓ → – . Double-click (or long-press) a cell to type your own text. Sign in to keep the list in sync across your devices; it is always saved on this device too, and Export CSV gives a backup.
